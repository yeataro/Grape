## Capability versus entrance

The exact toolbar position and menu placement are legacy reference configurations. The purpose, target, applicable conditions and observable results are the behaviors to preserve. Owner policy remains: current default-enabled experimental abilities are required, default-disabled variants are optional experimental. Renaming or moving an entrance cannot silently delete a required ability.

“Layout” in the new chapter means **node arrangement**. Workspace composition remains a separate reference chapter. Group creation/removal changes visual organization, not graph computation; body overlap alone does not grant membership. Link hide/show changes a view preference, not whether the data relation exists.
